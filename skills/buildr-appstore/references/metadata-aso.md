# App Store Optimization (ASO) & Metadata

---

## 1. Character Budgets

- **App Name**: Max **30 characters**.
- **Subtitle**: Max **30 characters**.
- **Keywords Field**: Max **100 characters** (comma-separated, no spaces after commas).
- **Promotional Text**: Max **170 characters** (can be updated anytime without a new binary submission).
- **Description**: Max **4,000 characters**.

---

## 2. Keyword Optimization Rules

1. **Do not repeat words**: If a word is in your App Name or Subtitle, Apple already indexes it. Do **not** repeat it in the keyword field.
2. **Single words over phrases**: Separate with commas: `fitness,tracker,workout,calories` (not `fitness tracker, workout tracker`).
3. **No category names or competitor trademarks**: Do not include "free", "app", or competitor names.

---

## 3. Screenshot Dimensions

- **iPhone 6.9" / 6.7" (iPhone 16 Pro Max, 15 Pro Max)**: `1320 x 2868` pixels or `1290 x 2796` pixels.
- **iPad Pro 13"**: `2064 x 2752` pixels.
